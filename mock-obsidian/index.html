Process

Slow work, on purpose.

  1. 01 · Inspect

    Measure

    Paint depth readings on every panel, so we never remove more than we should.

  2. 02 · Prepare

    Decontaminate

    Foam, hand wash, iron and clay. The paint is truly bare before polishing.

  3. 03 · Correct

    Refine

    Swirls, haze and scratches leveled under studio lighting, panel by panel.

  4. 04 · Protect

    Coat

    Ceramic applied and infrared-cured, then a final inspection with you.
